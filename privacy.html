Privacy Policy

LLC Ledger NAS Approvals · Effective 27 October 2026

What the app accesses

With the account owner's permission, the app connects to one Gmail account, 76everit@gmail.com, and uses it only to:

  • send purchase-approval request emails and decision notices (Gmail send permission);
  • read the reply thread of each approval request it sent, and find its own sent copies (Gmail read-only permission).

It does not read other mail, contacts, or any other Google data.

What it stores

The approval request, the approver's reply and the related email messages are stored on the owner's private server as a record of each decision. Nothing is stored with third parties.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and removal

Records are kept as long as they are needed for the business's accounts. To have records removed, or for any question, email 76everit@gmail.com. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
